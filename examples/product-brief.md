# Product Brief: RoomBuddy (fictional)

> Fictional material for the Agent Lab. Not based on any real company or project.

## Problem

A mid-sized office with 12 meeting rooms books rooms through a shared spreadsheet.
People double-book rooms, forget to cancel, and cannot see which rooms have a screen
or video equipment. Facility staff spend time every week fixing conflicts.

## Idea

A small web app, RoomBuddy, where employees can:

- see which rooms are free right now and later today
- book a room for a time slot
- cancel their own bookings
- filter rooms by size and equipment (screen, video conferencing, whiteboard)

Facility staff can:

- add, edit, or deactivate rooms
- see a weekly utilisation overview

## First release (MVP)

- single page that lists rooms and shows whether they are free right now
- booking and cancelling, no login yet (name field only)
- data can live in a JSON file

## Later

- login
- calendar integration
- recurring bookings
- auto-release of a room if nobody checks in within 10 minutes

## Constraints

- must work on a phone screen
- must be accessible (keyboard navigation, readable contrast)
